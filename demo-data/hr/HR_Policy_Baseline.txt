# GLOBAL HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY HANDBOOK (BASELINE v1.0)
Department: Human Resources (Tenant: HR)
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Document Reference: HR-POL-2024-001

1. Remote Work and Telecommuting Guidelines
Full-time corporate and technology personnel are permitted to work remotely for a maximum of two days per calendar week. All remote work arrangements require advance written authorization from the direct department manager. Core hours must be maintained regardless of physical location.

2. Core Operating Business Hours
The official standard operating hours for all corporate headquarters and regional delivery centers are established from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Eastern Standard Time, Monday through Friday. All staff must be reachable on corporate communication channels during these hours.

3. Annual Paid Time Off Accrual
All salaried regular full-time employees accrue paid time off (PTO) at the rate of 1.25 days per completed calendar month of active service, providing a total baseline allotment of fifteen (15) paid vacation days per calendar year.

4. Consecutive PTO Request Procedures
Any planned leave or paid vacation exceeding four (4) consecutive business days mandates formal submission through the human resources portal at least twenty-one (21) calendar days in advance of the planned absence.

5. Business Travel Expense Reimbursement
Reimbursement for meal expenditures during pre-approved domestic business travel is capped strictly at $75 per diem. Itemized tax receipts must be uploaded to the financial expense portal within thirty calendar days of travel completion.

6. Home Office Hardware Stipend
Newly hired remote or hybrid personnel are provided a one-time technology hardware reimbursement allowance not to exceed $500, applicable solely toward ergonomic seating, external monitors, or desktop peripherals.

7. Paid Parental Leave Provisions
Eligible primary caregivers following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child are granted twelve (12) consecutive weeks of fully compensated parental leave, funded at one hundred percent of base salary.

8. Secondary Caregiver Leave Policy
Secondary caregivers who have completed at least twelve continuous months of full-time enterprise service are eligible for four (4) consecutive weeks of fully compensated parental leave.

9. Compassionate Bereavement Leave
Employees experiencing the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling) are provided up to five (5) consecutive paid business days of compassionate bereavement leave.

10. Annual Performance Evaluation Cycle
The formal annual performance appraisal review cycle commences on November 1. All self-evaluations and manager performance ratings must be finalized and submitted to Human Resources before December 15.

11. Overtime Compensation for Non-Exempt Staff
Hourly non-exempt personnel who work in excess of forty (40) hours within a designated payroll workweek are compensated at one and one-half times (1.5x) their regular base hourly rate, subject to pre-approval.

12. Continuing Professional Education Tuition Assistance
The corporation provides eligible full-time employees up to $2,500 per calendar year in tuition and examination fee assistance for accredited graduate coursework or professional credentialing programs.

13. Enterprise Code of Business Conduct and Ethics
All team members must complete the mandatory annual corporate ethics and compliance refresher training by the conclusion of the first quarter (March 31) of each calendar year.

14. Workplace Harassment Prevention and Inclusivity
The enterprise enforces zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or retaliatory behaviors in any workplace interaction. Mandatory anti-harassment training must be completed bi-annually.

15. Employee Separation and Severance Policy
In instances of mutual separation or reduction in force, standard severance pay is calculated at one (1) week of base salary per completed year of continuous enterprise service, capped at twelve weeks total.
