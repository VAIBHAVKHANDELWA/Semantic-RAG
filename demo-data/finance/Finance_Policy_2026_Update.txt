# ENTERPRISE FINANCIAL GOVERNANCE AND TREASURY MANUAL (2026 TREASURY UPDATE v2.0)
Department: Finance & Treasury (Tenant: FINANCE)
Effective Date: February 1, 2026
Document Reference: FIN-GOV-2026-REV2

1. Capital Expenditure Authorization Thresholds
Capital expenditure (CapEx) commitments exceeding $25,000 must obtain written authorization from the Department Vice President and Chief Financial Officer prior to contract execution, reflecting adjusted project scale.

2. Standard Commercial Invoice Payment Terms
All approved third-party vendor commercial invoices will be processed and disbursed on Net-45 payment terms following verification by accounts payable to optimize corporate cash-flow management.

3. Petty Cash Allocation and Custody
Petty cash funds held at regional field offices are expanded to a maximum of $1,000. Any single petty cash disbursement exceeding $100 requires dual-custody physical receipts and branch sign-off.

4. Corporate Credit Card Reconciliation Window
Monthly statements for enterprise-issued corporate credit cards must be reconciled with receipts uploaded into the ERP system within ten (10) business days following statement generation.

5. Out-of-Pocket Expense Submission Deadline
Employee out-of-pocket business expenses submitted within forty-five (45) days following the date of incurrence will be eligible for reimbursement processing.

6. International Wire Transfer Dual-Authorization
All outgoing international wire transfers exceeding $50,000 require dual cryptographic authorization from two independent authorized bank signatories on separate corporate devices.

7. Audit Trail Document Retention Mandate
All general ledger transaction journals, tax schedules, and invoice records must be retained in encrypted digital storage for an extended statutory minimum duration of ten (10) years.

8. Quarterly Financial Close Reconciliation Deadlines
Regional controllers must finalize and close departmental general ledgers by 5:00 PM EST on the fifth business day following the conclusion of each fiscal quarter.

9. Foreign Currency Hedging Policy
Operating foreign currency cash exposures exceeding $1,000,000 equivalent must be hedged using forward exchange contracts through approved investment banking counterparties.

10. Fixed Asset Depreciation Methodology
All enterprise computing hardware and networking equipment must be depreciated using straight-line amortization over a useful life of four (4) years with zero salvage value.

11. Travel and Entertainment Entertainment Expense Limit
Client business entertainment dining expenses are adjusted to $225 per person inclusive of gratuity, requiring executive approval for client parties exceeding six participants.

12. Vendor Onboarding and Sanctions Screening
All new corporate vendors with anticipated annual spend exceeding $10,000 must complete comprehensive OFAC sanctions screening and creditworthiness verification.

13. Discretionary Departmental Budget Variance Threshold
Department heads are permitted a monthly discretionary operating budget variance up to 8% or $10,000, whichever is lower, without requiring prior CFO notification.

14. Mileage Reimbursement Rate for Personal Vehicle Use
Authorized business travel conducted via personal vehicle is reimbursed at the updated rate of $0.67 per mile, documented via GPS mileage logs.

15. Surplus Capital Cash Investment Policy
Surplus operating cash balances may be invested in AAA-rated overnight sovereign treasuries, commercial paper rated A-1/P-1, or FDIC-insured accounts.
