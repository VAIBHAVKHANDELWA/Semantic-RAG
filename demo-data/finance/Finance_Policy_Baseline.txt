# ENTERPRISE FINANCIAL GOVERNANCE AND TREASURY MANUAL (BASELINE v1.0)
Department: Finance & Treasury (Tenant: FINANCE)
Effective Date: March 1, 2024
Document Reference: FIN-GOV-2024-01

1. Capital Expenditure Authorization Thresholds
Capital expenditure (CapEx) commitments exceeding $10,000 must obtain written authorization from the Department Vice President and Chief Financial Officer prior to contract execution or purchase order issuance.

2. Standard Commercial Invoice Payment Terms
All approved third-party vendor commercial invoices will be processed and disbursed strictly on Net-30 payment terms following verification by accounts payable and receipt of verified goods.

3. Petty Cash Allocation and Custody
Petty cash funds held at regional field offices are capped at $500. Any single petty cash disbursement exceeding $50 requires dual-custody physical receipts and management sign-off.

4. Corporate Credit Card Reconciliation Window
Monthly statements for enterprise-issued corporate credit cards must be reconciled with receipts uploaded into the ERP system within five (5) business days following statement generation.

5. Out-of-Pocket Expense Submission Deadline
Employee out-of-pocket business expenses submitted later than thirty (30) days following the date of incurrence will be deemed non-reimbursable without exception.

6. International Wire Transfer Dual-Authorization
All outgoing international wire transfers exceeding $25,000 require dual cryptographic authorization from two independent authorized bank signatories on separate corporate devices.

7. Audit Trail Document Retention Mandate
All general ledger transaction journals, tax schedules, and invoice records must be retained in encrypted digital storage for a statutory minimum duration of seven (7) years.

8. Quarterly Financial Close Reconciliation Deadlines
Regional controllers must finalize and close departmental general ledgers by 5:00 PM EST on the third business day following the conclusion of each fiscal quarter.

9. Foreign Currency Hedging Policy
Operating foreign currency cash exposures exceeding $500,000 equivalent must be hedged using forward exchange contracts through approved investment banking counterparties.

10. Fixed Asset Depreciation Methodology
All enterprise computing hardware and networking equipment must be depreciated using straight-line amortization over a useful life of three (3) years with zero salvage value.

11. Travel and Entertainment Entertainment Expense Limit
Client business entertainment dining expenses are capped at $150 per person inclusive of gratuity, requiring executive approval for client parties exceeding four participants.

12. Vendor Onboarding and Sanctions Screening
All new corporate vendors with anticipated annual spend exceeding $5,000 must complete comprehensive OFAC sanctions screening and creditworthiness verification.

13. Discretionary Departmental Budget Variance Threshold
Department heads are permitted a monthly discretionary operating budget variance not to exceed 5% or $5,000, whichever is lower, without CFO notification.

14. Mileage Reimbursement Rate for Personal Vehicle Use
Authorized business travel conducted via personal vehicle is reimbursed at the baseline rate of $0.58 per mile, documented via GPS mileage logs.

15. Surplus Capital Cash Investment Policy
Surplus operating cash balances must be invested strictly in AAA-rated overnight sovereign treasury repurchase agreements or FDIC-insured certificates of deposit.
